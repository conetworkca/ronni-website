HER STORY

A coach who has done this from both sides.

Ronni spent 25 years in corporate leadership before deciding her expertise deserved more room than a single employer could offer. Rather than leaving on faith, she built what she needed first — a validated consulting offer, real income proof, and a plan for the transition — and only then made the move.

That order matters. It's the foundation of everything Career Freedom Catalyst teaches.

Today, Ronni holds ICF credentials, has logged over 500 hours coaching accomplished professionals through career transitions, and has worked directly with women across STEM, healthcare, and professional services. Her approach pairs the emotional intelligence required to navigate a real identity shift with the structure required to build something real.

She built Career Freedom Catalyst because she noticed the same pattern again and again: talented, capable women staying in roles that no longer fit them — not for lack of ability, but for lack of a responsible plan for what came next.
